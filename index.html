🏫 Hector Garcia Middle School

📍 School Information

School Name: Hector Garcia Middle School

Location: San Antonio, Texas

Grade Levels: 6-8

Type: Public Middle School

Description: Hector Garcia Middle School is a leading educational institution in San Antonio dedicated to fostering academic excellence and student development. The school provides a nurturing environment where students can grow intellectually, socially, and creatively. With a focus on STEM education and arts programs, Hector Garcia prepares students for high school and beyond.

🎵 Band Program

Program Overview: The Hector Garcia Middle School Band Program is a premier music education initiative that develops talented young musicians. Students learn discipline, teamwork, and musical excellence through rigorous training and performance opportunities.

Grade Levels: Open to students in grades 6-8

Instruments Offered: Woodwinds (Flute, Clarinet, Saxophone, Oboe), Brass (Trumpet, Trombone, French Horn, Tuba), Percussion (Drums, Timpani, Mallets, Cymbals)

Program Highlights:

🏆 Competitions

Regular participation in UIL competitions and music festivals throughout Texas

🎼 Performances

Concert performances, pep rallies, football games, and community events

👨‍🏫 Experienced Directors

Dedicated band directors with advanced music education degrees

🎓 Music Theory

Comprehensive music theory and sight-reading instruction

Benefits: Students develop musicianship, discipline, time management, and collaboration skills. The band program builds confidence and provides lifelong memories of musical achievement.
